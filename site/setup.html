Download for Windows

These early installers aren’t signed yet. Your computer may show a security warning. See how to open the preview.

  1. Open your download

    Open the installer in Downloads. Click Install, then leave “Open Alfred” selected and click Finish.

  2. Connect the AI you use

    Choose Claude or ChatGPT / Codex. Click Install & connect. Alfred prepares the connection and opens the provider’s sign-in page for you.

  3. Let Alfred help

    Follow the permission checks, then click Start Alfred. Try “Open my browser.” Stay nearby while it works.

You need an eligible AI account and an internet connection. Your provider’s usage limits and charges still apply.

Can I install this on a phone or tablet?

Alfred is a desktop app. Open this page on your Windows PC, Mac, or supported Linux computer to install it.

Already have Claude or Codex installed?

Alfred detects it. Click Check connection in the app to confirm your sign-in, then continue. You do not need to reinstall it.
