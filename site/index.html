// the help desk

questions, answered.

he takes a look at your screen, decides the next move, and controls the real mouse and keyboard to carry it out — then looks again to check it worked. the same way you'd do it, just faster and without the sighing.

Alfred takes screenshots during your tasks and sends them to your chosen AI provider. Stay nearby while it works. Click Stop in the app to stop before the next action after the current AI response returns.

choose Claude or ChatGPT / Codex. Alfred helps install the connection and opens browser sign-in. An Anthropic API key is also available as an advanced option. Provider limits and charges apply.

this preview can make mistakes. The model is instructed to ask before sensitive actions, but that is not a guaranteed safeguard. Supervise tasks involving messages, files or purchases.
